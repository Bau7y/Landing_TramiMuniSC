## Who we are

TramiMuniSC is a **private service** that assists with procedures before the Municipality of San Carlos, Alajuela, Costa Rica. **It is not the Municipality of San Carlos, is not part of it and does not act on its behalf.** Contact: [tramimuni.sc@gmail.com](mailto:tramimuni.sc@gmail.com).

## Scope of the service

We help you gather and check the requirements, fill in the forms and file the procedure. The decision on each request always belongs to the municipality, so we cannot guarantee an outcome. Resolution times depend on the municipality.

## Information on this site

The guides, FAQs and procedure descriptions are general information based on the regulations in force on the date shown on each page. They do not replace a municipal resolution or legal advice for your case. Laws and requirements may change.

## Official forms

The downloadable forms are documents of the Municipality of San Carlos, published on its [official site](https://www.munisc.go.cr/Paginas/Visitantes/Documentos.aspx). We only added fillable fields to make them easier to complete; their content was not changed.

## Responsibility for documents

The person requesting the procedure is responsible for the data and documents they provide being true and current.
