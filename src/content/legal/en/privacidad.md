This policy explains how TramiMuniSC handles the personal data it receives through this site, under Costa Rica's Ley N.° 8968 on the Protection of Individuals regarding the Processing of their Personal Data.

## Controller

TramiMuniSC, a private service that assists with municipal procedures, based in San Carlos, Alajuela, Costa Rica. Contact: [tramimuni.sc@gmail.com](mailto:tramimuni.sc@gmail.com).

## What data we collect

- **Contact form:** name, email, subject, type of procedure, message and, if you attach it, the completed form or a photo. That file may include your ID number, the property number and other property details.
- **Technical data:** the minimum needed to prevent automated or abusive form submissions. It is not used to identify you.
- **Visit statistics:** visits are counted in aggregate, without identifying anyone.

## Cookies

This site does not use cookies or tracking or advertising tools. Your name and the light or dark theme you choose are saved only in your browser for convenience; they never reach us and you can delete them by clearing the site's data.

## Why we use it

Only to answer your request and, if you ask us to, handle your procedure before the Municipality of San Carlos. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or share it with third parties beyond the services that run the site.

Before sending the form, you tick the box accepting this policy. That consent is recorded in the message we receive, together with the version of the policy you accepted.

## Who else is involved

To run the site we use web hosting, email delivery and anti-spam verification providers. They only process data on our behalf and for those purposes. Some of them store information on servers outside Costa Rica.

## How long we keep it

As long as needed to answer your request and follow up on the procedure. After that we delete it, unless a law requires us to keep it.

## Your rights

You can ask at any time to **access**, **correct** or **delete** your data, and **revoke your consent**, by writing to [tramimuni.sc@gmail.com](mailto:tramimuni.sc@gmail.com). If you believe your rights were not respected, you can contact Costa Rica's Data Protection Agency (PRODHAB).

Providing your data is voluntary, but without it we cannot answer your request.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with its update date.
