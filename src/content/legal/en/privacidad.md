This policy explains how TramiMuniSC handles the personal data it receives through this site, under Costa Rica's Ley N.° 8968 on the Protection of Individuals regarding the Processing of their Personal Data.

## Controller

TramiMuniSC, a private service that assists with municipal procedures, based in San Carlos, Alajuela, Costa Rica. Contact: [tramimuni.sc@gmail.com](mailto:tramimuni.sc@gmail.com).

## What data we collect

- **Contact form:** name, email, subject, type of procedure, message and, if you attach it, the completed form or a photo. That file may include your ID number, the property number and other property details.
- **Technical data:** the IP address of whoever sends the form, used only to limit mass submissions, and Cloudflare Turnstile's anti-spam check.
- **Visit statistics:** Vercel Web Analytics counts visits in aggregate, without cookies and without identifying you.
- **On your device:** your name is saved in the browser so you don't have to type it again, along with the light or dark theme you choose. This data is not sent anywhere and you can delete it by clearing the site's data.

## Why we use it

Only to answer your request and, if you ask us to, handle your procedure before the Municipality of San Carlos. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or share it with third parties beyond the services that run the site.

By sending the form, you consent to this use.

## Services involved

- **Vercel:** site hosting and visit statistics.
- **Resend:** delivery of the form message to our inbox.
- **Google (Gmail):** receiving and answering emails.
- **Cloudflare Turnstile:** checking that the submission is not automated.

Some of these services store information on servers outside Costa Rica.

## How long we keep it

As long as needed to answer your request and follow up on the procedure. After that we delete it, unless a law requires us to keep it.

## Your rights

You can ask at any time to **access**, **correct** or **delete** your data, and withdraw your consent, by writing to [tramimuni.sc@gmail.com](mailto:tramimuni.sc@gmail.com). If you believe your rights were not respected, you can contact Costa Rica's Data Protection Agency (PRODHAB).

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with its update date.
