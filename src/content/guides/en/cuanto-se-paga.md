## How it is calculated

The Municipality of San Carlos collects the property tax under Ley N.° 7509. The rate is the same for every property: **0.25% of the registered value**.

The registered value is the one the municipality holds in its database for your property. It includes the land and the fixed, permanent buildings or installations, such as the house, a warehouse or a water tank.

**Example:** a property with a registered value of ₡30,000,000 pays ₡75,000 a year (30,000,000 × 0.0025), that is, ₡18,750 per quarter.

## When it is paid

The tax is annual: the period starts on January 1 and ends on December 31 (Ley 7509, art. 22). For collection, the yearly amount is split into four quarters. An unpaid quarter accrues interest.

## Where the registered value comes from

The value is updated in several ways:

- **Your declaration**, which you must file at least every five years (art. 16).
- **A valuation by the municipality**, for example when there is no current declaration.
- **A sale, mortgage or other entry** recorded in the National Registry.

If the value does not match your property, the way to correct it is to file an updated declaration. If the municipality notifies you of a valuation you disagree with, you can appeal it within the deadline stated in the notice.

## How to pay less, legally

- **Single-property exemption:** if the property is the only one an individual owns nationwide, no tax is paid on the first 45 base salaries of its value; only on the excess (art. 4, item e). It is requested every year.
- **Agricultural land:** Ley N.° 9071 allows it to be valued with the agricultural value platform, which usually gives a lower value than an urban lot.
- **Declare accurate data:** a wrong area or building type changes the amount.
