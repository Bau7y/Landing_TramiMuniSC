## What it is

Ley N.° 7509 (art. 4, item e) states that an individual's **only property**, whether or not it is recorded in the National Registry, pays no property tax up to a value of **45 base salaries**. If the property is worth more, tax is paid only on the difference.

The base salary is set every year by the Judiciary, so the cap changes from year to year.

## Who can apply

- An **individual** (it does not apply to companies).
- Who owns **only one property in the whole country**, not just in San Carlos.
- Who is the **registered owner**, or the possessor if the property is not recorded.

If you own two or more properties, even if one is a small lot, you do not qualify.

## Requirements at the Municipality of San Carlos

- Application form filled in and signed by the owner, with no erasures or crossed-out text.
- Valid Costa Rican ID. Foreigners: residence card or passport.
- Property declaration up to date.
- Municipal taxes up to date (Ley 4755, arts. 18 and 18 bis).

## When to apply

The exemption is not permanent: it is requested **every year**. Since the tax is charged from January to December, the best time to file is **January**, so it covers the whole period from the first quarter.

## Common mistakes

- Filing with an unpaid quarter: the request is rejected until it is paid.
- Forgetting to renew it: the next year the full tax is charged again.
- Signing on someone else's behalf: the owner or their legal representative must sign.
