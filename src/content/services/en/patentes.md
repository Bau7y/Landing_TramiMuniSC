Individuals and companies carrying out a **for-profit activity** in the canton of San Carlos need a municipal license and pay the business tax, under Costa Rica’s Ley N.° 7773.

To grant, relocate or transfer a license, the municipality requires your **municipal taxes to be up to date**. We help you gather the requirements and file a complete application.
