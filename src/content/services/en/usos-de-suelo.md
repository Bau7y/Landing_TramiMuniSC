The land use certificate is the municipal document stating whether the activity you plan —housing, retail, industry or other— **complies with the zoning of the Plan Regulador** (zoning plan) of San Carlos.

It is usually required before a **building permit** or a **business license (patente)**, and for some procedures with other institutions.
